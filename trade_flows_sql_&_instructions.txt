🔵 Running this command will bring up data from 2000 to 2005.
(Caught a year late)

SELECT * FROM analysis.trade_flows
WHERE year >= '2000' AND year < '2006';

🔵Skips the first 39 rows and starts displaying data from row 40. (Edit the numbers however you like)

SELECT * FROM "analysis"."trade_flows"
LIMIT 50 OFFSET 39;
